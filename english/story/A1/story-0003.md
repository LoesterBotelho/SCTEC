Story 4 — “New Days in the City”

Chapter 1: Early Morning at Home

The alarm rings at 6:00 AM.

Daniel opens his eyes slowly. He feels tired because he slept late yesterday.

His mother is in the kitchen making eggs and tea.

“Good morning,” she says with a smile.

Daniel sits at the table and says:
“I don’t want to go to work today.”

His younger sister Emma laughs.
“You say that every Monday!”

Daniel drinks water and checks his phone.

His friend Noah sends a message:
“Don’t forget the meeting today.”

Daniel suddenly remembers.
“Oh no! I have an important presentation.”

He starts thinking:
I must stay calm. I can do this.

🚌 Chapter 2: Bus Ride and Small Conversations

The bus is crowded and noisy.

A man is listening to music loudly.
Two students are talking about video games.

Daniel finds a seat near the window.

An old woman smiles and asks:
“Are you going to work?”

“Yes,” Daniel answers politely.
“I work at a technology company.”

“That sounds interesting,” she says.

Daniel looks outside and imagines his future.

Maybe one day I will create my own company.
Perhaps I will work in another country.

The bus suddenly stops.

Everyone moves forward quickly.

A child starts crying.

Daniel helps the woman with her bags.

“Thank you,” she says kindly.

“You’re welcome,” Daniel replies.

Chapter 3: Problems at Work

At the office, people are typing, answering calls, and drinking coffee.

Daniel’s manager, Mrs. Carter, looks worried.

“The clients are arriving in thirty minutes,” she says.
“We still have problems with the software.”

Daniel sits at his computer and checks the system carefully.

His colleague Mia says:
“The database is slower than yesterday.”

Daniel thinks deeply.

Why is the server responding so slowly?
Did someone change the configuration?

After several minutes, he finds the problem.

“I found it!” he says excitedly.
“One file was corrupted during the update.”

Mrs. Carter smiles with relief.
“Excellent work, Daniel.”

During the presentation, the system works perfectly.

The clients clap at the end.

Daniel feels proud and thinks:
Today started badly, but now everything feels better.

Chapter 4: Evening with Friends

After work, Daniel meets Noah and Emma at a small café.

Noah says:
“You looked very stressed this morning.”

“I was,” Daniel admits.
“But things improved.”

Emma is eating chocolate cake slowly.

“You always solve problems,” she says.
“You think too much, but that helps sometimes.”

Daniel laughs.

The café is calm.
Soft music is playing in the background.

Outside, rain begins to fall.

Noah looks at the window and says:
“I wish life could slow down sometimes.”

Daniel answers quietly:
“Maybe life is difficult because we are always chasing something.”

For a moment, nobody speaks.

Then Emma smiles and says:
“Or maybe we just need better cake.”

Everyone laughs again.

Daniel thinks:
Family and friends make difficult days easier.
