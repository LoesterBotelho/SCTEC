Story 4 — “The Dream Beyond the Screen”

Chapter 1: A Late Start

Sophie wakes up late on Saturday morning.

Her room is messy.
Books, headphones, and clothes are everywhere.

Her father knocks on the door.

“You should clean this room today,” he says.

Sophie stretches and replies:
“I know, Dad. I will do it later.”

She goes downstairs and prepares cereal with milk.

Her cousin Lily is visiting the house.

Lily says:
“Do you want to go to the shopping center today?”

Sophie smiles.
“Yes! I need new shoes.”

Inside her mind, Sophie thinks:
I also want to buy a notebook for my ideas.

Chapter 2: Shopping and Imagination

The shopping center is full of people.

Children are running.
Music is playing loudly from the stores.

Sophie and Lily enter a bookstore.

Sophie sees a book about digital art and animation.

“This looks amazing,” she says.

Lily asks:
“Do you still want to become a designer?”

“Yes,” Sophie answers immediately.
“I love creating characters and stories.”

She opens the book carefully.

The pictures show futuristic cities, robots, and flying cars.

Sophie starts imagining:
What if I create games one day?
What if people around the world play them?

Lily smiles and says:
“You dream a lot.”

“Maybe,” Sophie replies.
“But dreams can become plans.”

🎧 Chapter 3: Study, Creativity, and Doubts

At night, Sophie sits at her desk with her laptop.

She is watching design tutorials and practicing drawing.

Her friend Ethan calls her.

“Are you still studying?” he asks.

“Yes,” Sophie replies.
“I want to improve before college starts.”

Ethan says:
“You work very hard. Don’t forget to rest.”

Sophie stops drawing for a moment.

Sometimes she feels uncertain about the future.

What if I fail?
What if I am not talented enough?

Then she remembers something her teacher once said:

“Skill grows with practice.”

Sophie opens a new project and continues working.

After two hours, she finishes a colorful digital illustration.

She smiles proudly.

“I’m getting better,” she whispers.

Chapter 4: Quiet Thoughts Before Sleep

Later that night, Sophie lies in bed looking at the ceiling.

Rain is falling softly outside.

Her phone lights up with a message from Lily:

“You are going to do great things someday.”

Sophie smiles warmly.

She thinks about her family, her future, and her dreams.

Life still feels confusing sometimes.

But she understands something important:

People do not discover their future in one day.
They build it slowly, step by step.

She closes her eyes peacefully and thinks:

Tomorrow is another chance to learn something new.
