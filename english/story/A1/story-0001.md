🌅 Daily Life Story: “A Week of Changes”
☀️ Scene 1: Morning at Home

It is Monday morning. The sun is bright and the house is quiet.

Anna wakes up early. She says:
“Mom, I am tired. I want to sleep more.”

Her mother answers:
“You have to get up. You must go to school.”

Anna says:
“I can’t. I feel sleepy.”

Her brother Leo is already eating breakfast.
He is drinking coffee and watching the news.

Leo says:
“You should eat something. You might feel better.”

Anna says:
“I always feel hungry in the morning, but today I feel strange.”

🏫 Scene 2: School Time

At school, Anna meets her friend Julia.

Julia says:
“Did you do the homework?”

Anna answers:
“I did it yesterday. It was easy.”

The teacher enters and says:
“We are going to study English verbs today.”

Anna is writing and thinking:
“I want to speak English well. I hope I can do it.”

The teacher says:
“You must practice every day. You can improve fast.”

Julia whispers:
“I sometimes forget my homework, but I am trying.”

⚽ Scene 3: Sports Practice

After school, Anna and Leo go to football practice.

The coach says:
“You must run faster. You can do better!”

Leo is running and says:
“I am going to win this match!”

Anna says:
“I might be tired, but I will try.”

They are playing together. It is raining a little, but they continue.

Leo falls and says:
“I fell yesterday too, but I am okay now.”

Anna says:
“You should be careful.”

💼 Scene 4: Office / Work Place

Their father is working in an office.

He says:
“I have worked here for five years.”

His colleague says:
“You have improved a lot.”

He is sending emails and talking on the phone.

He thinks:
“I want a better position. I hope I get a promotion.”

His boss says:
“You must finish this report today.”

He replies:
“I will do it. I can complete it.”

🍽️ Scene 5: Dinner with Family and Friends

At night, the family has dinner.

The mother says:
“We are going to eat together every night now.”

Anna says:
“I love this moment. I always feel happy here.”

Julia visits the house.

Julia says:
“I might stay for dinner if that is okay.”

Leo jokes:
“You never eat slowly!”

Everyone laughs.

Anna thinks:
“I wish every day was like this. I hope we stay together forever.”

Her father says:
“We have been very busy, but now we are together.”
